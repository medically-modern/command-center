### 5.30l Calendly bookings link to a chart by PHONE too (Sep 2026)
Josh, 2026-09-30: *"when people are filling out the calendly its not linking to their profiles in
welcome call / intake … they rarely have email … can we require phone number in calendly?"*

**Why nothing linked.** Every place a Calendly booking meets a chart joined on the invitee's
**email** and nothing else: the day strip's "Open" (`scheduleEntries.bookingLinker`), both columns'
Scheduled/Unscheduled split (`useCalendlyBookings` → `workflow.intakeBooking` /
`welcomeCallBuckets`), and the Welcome Call "Call scheduled" chip (§5.31e). Measured on the live
board 2026-09-30: **10 of 32** Welcome Call patients had an email (`text_mm1xc140`) and **32 of 32**
had a phone (`phone_mm1x44yk`). §5.31e's "6 of 6 carried an email" measured the intake form
population, not this one. The Calendly read itself was healthy (the gateway's
`/calendly/patient/health` showed 4 indexed bookings and nothing unresolved); the join was the
failure. The intake event type collected no phone at all (`needs_invitee_phone: false`).

**The chain, and what each hop now does:**
- **Calendly**: the booking page must COLLECT a phone (setup below). Nothing links until it does.
- **dtc-mm-form** (`server/src/calendly.js`): `inviteePhone(event, invitee, customQuestions)` reads,
  in order, (1) the event's `location` when its type is `outbound_call` (Calendly's "Phone call",
  host calls invitee), (2) the answer to a custom question whose TYPE is phone, matched by the
  question's text against the event type's `custom_questions`, (3) `text_reminder_number`. Each
  must pass `phone.isValidUsPhone`; the result is ten digits or `''`. `dayEvents` puts it on every
  booking as `phone`. ⚠️ `inbound_call` is OUR number and is never read. ⚠️ A question is never
  picked by its wording ("Your doctor's office phone?" says phone and is someone else's).
- **Gateway** (`calendlyPatientRules.phoneKey` / `indexByPhone` / `lookupManyPhones`): the
  window index is keyed by phone as well as email. `POST /calendly/patients` takes
  `{emails?, phones?, kind}` and answers `bookings` (by email, unchanged) plus `byPhone`;
  `GET /calendly/patient` takes `email` and/or `phone`, answers on the email first, and says
  `matchedBy`. `/calendly/day` needed no change: it passes bookings through.
- **SPA**: `lib/shared/phoneCell.bookingPhoneKey` is the one key (ten digits or `''`, never a
  truncation). It lives in `phoneCell` because the hook tests mock the Calendly client wholesale.

**The rules, and why each is the way it is:**
- ⚠️ **The strip (`byContact` in `scheduleEntries.ts`)**: the email join behaves EXACTLY as it
  did before phones (a unique email links, a shared one links nobody), and the phone only adds a
  link where the email found no row at all. When the email names one patient and the phone names a
  DIFFERENT one, nobody is linked, because a wrong chart on a live call is worse than no chart
  (§5.28). A number two rows share (a household line, a duplicate lead) links neither
  (`phoneIndex` poisons it, like `emailIndex`). The intake event-URI join still wins outright.
- ⚠️ **The columns ask only `phonesHeldOnce(rows)`**: a number two rows of that column share is
  never asked, so one household booking can't mark two patients Scheduled and drop the unbooked one
  out of Unscheduled (the call nobody makes). `calendlyAnswerFor` then takes email first, phone
  second. A number ABSENT from `byPhone` (shared, or an older gateway that ignores `phones`) is "not
  asked", never "not booked": `intakeBooking` branches 2/3 still fall back to the mirror.
- **The chip** asks by email and phone. There is no queue in hand there to poison a shared number,
  so when the match came by phone the chip's hover says so and names who the booking is under.
- **An unmatched block** shows the phone given on the booking as plain text, "(given on the
  booking)" (`ScheduleEntry.bookedPhone`), and does NOT dial it: `phone` stays the matched row's,
  and Log call attempt still needs a chart.

**Prefill.** The phone we already hold is prefilled into the booking link so the number the booking
is matched on is ours: `location=<ten digits>`, the parameter Calendly documents for the Phone call
location. It goes out ONLY when dtc-mm-form reports that the event type's one location is Phone call
(`phoneCaptureOf(et).prefill === "location"`, served on `GET /api/intake/scheduling` as
`phone_prefill` and `welcome.phone_prefill`). Both senders do it: the form's own embed
(`index.html mountCalendly`) and `BookingLinkDialog` (the row's phone, or in text mode the number
being texted). ⚠️ No prefill for a custom phone question: Calendly does not document which `aN` a
question takes, and a guess could show the number to the patient under someone else's question.

**The Calendly setup (done in Calendly's UI, per event type, not in code).** On the
`records-medicallymodern` account, for **Medically Modern Intake Call** and **Medically Modern
Welcome Call**: event type → Location → replace the "Custom: Phone call, Medically Modern will call
you." location with **Phone call**, choose the option that makes Calendly **require the invitee's
phone number** (the host calls the invitee, not "invitee should call me"), and leave it as the ONLY
location (with two, the patient can pick the one without a phone). A required custom question with
answer type "Phone number" also works for matching, but gets no prefill.
**State on 2026-09-30, read off the live health report right after deploy:** the **Welcome Call**
event type was ALREADY set up this way (`locations: ["outbound_call"]`, `required: true`,
`prefill: "location"`), so welcome bookings carry a phone from now on; the **Intake Call** still had
its one `custom` location (`required: false`), so intake bookings carry none until it is changed.
**Check it from outside**: `GET https://dtc-mm-form-api-production.up.railway.app/api/calendly/health`
reports `event_type.phone_capture` and `welcome_event_type.phone_capture` (`required: true`,
`prefill: "location"` is the target), and the gateway's `/calendly/patient/health` reports
`indexedPhones` (distinct booking phones in the 21-day window; 0 until bookings carry one). Both are
counts and config, no PHI.

**Not changed, on purpose:** the monday MIRROR (dtc-mm-form `booking.findPatientRow`, the intake
webhook and `reconcileDay`) still joins on email, so the ten-minute reminder (which reads the mirror)
and `/api/intake/reschedule-link` still miss a booking made under another address. Extending the
mirror to phone is a write to Profile Send Off from a webhook and was left for a separate decision.
Bookings made before the setup carry no phone and stay email-only.

**Back-compat and deploy order:** every hop tolerates the one before it being old. No `phone` on a
booking → empty phone index; no `byPhone` in an answer → empty map → "not asked". So the three can
deploy in any order. ⚠️ `/api/calendly/day` and `/calendly/day` now carry phone numbers: both were
already PHI routes behind their tokens (§5.30b), and nothing logs a booking body.
