**2. "Make obvious what's in each shipment — shipment 1 of 2 and 2 of 2 are confusing."**
⚠️⚠️ **§5.51c's premise was wrong: the board DOES hold per-line shipment data.** The **Line Item
Detail** column (`long_text_mm489t0z`, written by cardinal-api-poller on every status poll, and
already in the order's DETAIL read) lists every SKU with its status and the tracking number(s)
it shipped under. Measured on the live board — the order Brandon pointed at read:
`L2 <infusion set SKU> x3 -> Backordered (BO: 3)` / `L1 <cartridge SKU> x3 -> SHIPPED` /
`SHIP FedEx <tracking> qty 3 …`. So the card drew "Shipment 1 of 2" (the cartridges, unnamed)
and "Shipment 2 of 2" (a backorder that is not a shipment at all), with the items listed once,
apart from both.
- **`lib/orders/lineItems.ts`** parses it; **`patient/orderCard.orderShipmentsFromLines`** turns
  it into parcels WITH their items and one **Not shipped yet** block that is never numbered as a
  shipment. Names come from the Cardinal SKU Tracker by SKU (`skuLabel`, one shared 30-minute
  read, §5.35); an unknown SKU prints as the code.
- The shapes, each a test: a SHIP record echoed under both halves of a split line is counted ONCE;
  `9999` (Cardinal's hold line) and `00MMWELCOME` (`xnull`, no parcel) are not products; a line
  Cardinal DROPPED (`SUBSTITUTED (dropped by Cardinal): …`) is still owed until a `SUBSTITUTION
  ORDER` section exists, whose lines then ship in their own parcel marked *substitute for …*.
- ⚠️ **The text can be OLDER than the tracking columns** (it is stamped; a different poll writes
  tracking). A tracking number it does not list becomes a parcel whose contents "aren't in
  Cardinal's line list yet (last updated …)" — never guessed.
- ⚠️ **Fallback, not override:** no line list, a pre-tracking order, or Cardinal's verdict saying
  something is still to come while the list says nothing is → `orderShipments`, the old view,
  exactly. A pending block Cardinal asserts is never hidden by a stale list.
- A partially shipped order's ONE parcel with a Delivery Date now says *Delivered <date>*, not
  *In transit* — the order's Delivery Date can only be that parcel's.
- Also fixed on the card while rendering it: the grey *"Swapping a backordered set … needs Adjust
  orders"* notice split into three columns (`.notice` is a flex row; bare text beside a `<b>`).

**4. "Why isn't [a patient] matching on welcome call profile — booked but we don't have her on any

**only 8 of the 32 Welcome Call rows had an email** — so an unmatched booking is the common case,
not an anomaly. Shipped:
- ⚠️ **A matched welcome booking showed "Patient phone: Not on file" and could not be dialled** —
  `CareCoordinatorPage.welcomeItems` handed the strip only `{id, email}`. It now carries the phone,
  the group and the name.
- **A booking that matched nobody offers a POSSIBLE match by NAME** (`scheduleEntries.nameIndex` /
  `welcomeNameSuggester`: first + last word, accents, punctuation and initials ignored; a name two
  rows share suggests nobody). ⚠️ It is a hint in the popup — *"Possible match by name: X … confirm
  it's the same person (date of birth, phone) before you call"* — and NEVER feeds `href`, the
  phone or Log call attempt: a name is not identity (§5.28's `nameMatchAccepted` rule).
- The unmatched sentence now says what to do: nobody on the queue has the booking's email or name
  — they may have booked with a caregiver's email or another name; search by name.
- Not built, worth deciding: collecting a phone on the welcome Calendly form (then a phone join),
  and a manual "link this booking to a patient".

**6. "Already in System — surface one sentence conclusion … pulled from the notes."**
`lib/profile/dupCheckSummary.ts` + `components/shared/InSystemTooltip.tsx`.
- **The source is Profile Send Off Notes (`text_mm389fs`)**, where the `duplicate-patient-check`
  automation (josh-monday-automations) writes its write-up in one fixed order: `[stamp] Duplicate
  Check: <label>` · optional `⚠️ DIFFERENT SERVING:` · Linked by / Confidence / Current status ·
  optional documents · Pipeline history · Changes · `Recommended:` · Claude's 3–6 sentence summary ·
  optional `Patient UID:` · `—Claude`. ⚠️ **Not Dup Check Analysis (`long_text_mm651jfa`)**: a
  long_text, every value cut at 2,000 characters — and the conclusion is the END of the note.
- **The pick:** a different-serving line wins; else the summary's first sentence if it states a
  verdict (duplicate / new order / nothing new / treat it as…); else the LAST summary sentence that
  does (a third of the write-ups lead with the identity match); else Recommended's first sentence.
  Capped at 220 characters. The newest write-up wins, and a rep's note appended after it is not
  read. Measured by the research pass on all 28 write-ups on the board: every one yields a sentence.
- **Where it shows** (a Radix tooltip, 150ms, portalled — reps reported native `title` tooltips
  "not showing", `FaxStatusBadge`): the Care Coordinator card's pill (notes it already reads),
  Profile Send-Off's header pill, Patient Intake's header pill, and inline in Patient Intake's red
  *Already In System* banner. ⚠️ **The intake SIDEBAR pill says the verdict and points at the
  header**: the list read does not carry the notes column and must not (`listColumns.test.ts`).
- Never blank: no write-up → what the verdict means (a partial lead is flagged, never written up);
