**6. "Log call attempt" reaches every call — post and during** (Josh, same evening:
*"what happened on the call? doesnt write anywhere / not sure follow up on is writing
anywhere either / make sure log call attempt here is wired up to work post and during
call"*).

**The writers were never broken — verified against the live boards first.** Both boards'
activity logs for 2026-09-25 show every logged attempt landing all three columns: intake
Attempt Counter `numeric_mm5ze82q` + Follow Up Date `date_mm3874an` + the stamped
`Call attempt N — <note>` line in Notes `text_mm389fs` (including Josh's own 4:15 PM ET
test on the "TEST TEsT" item), and Welcome Call's `text_mm322fg9` / `date_mm38a7k7` /
`text_mm6vqq2k` the same. "Doesn't write anywhere" was about REACH, not the writes: the
form itself was unreachable from most of the dashboard's call paths.

The holes, all in how the one attempt form (`CallPatientDialog`) could be opened:
1. **The day strip's booking popup — the scheduled call, the coordinator's main call —
   had NO log step at all.** `BookingDetailsDialog` dialed through a bare
   `DialPatientDialog` (no `onLogAttempt`), so a call made from a booking block offered
   nowhere to say what happened, during or after.
2. **Post-call from a card, the only road back to the form was Call — which placed a
   whole new call.**
3. **Reopening the form mid-call redialed** on top of the live call (the dial-on-open
   effect knew only the item id).

The fix, kept to the one form so the two boards' writers stay single (`callAttempt.ts`'s
own rule):
- `CallTarget.dial?: boolean` — `false` opens `CallPatientDialog` as the attempt form
  alone; and the dial effect now also skips when a call to that number is already live.
- `ScheduleEntry.itemId` (the same resolution `href`/`phone` come from, never a guess);
  `BookingDetailsDialog` gets a **Log call attempt** button (post-call) and passes
  `onLogAttempt` into its dial popup (during); `ScheduleGrid` threads the page's
  handler through. The page's `logFromBooking` looks the row up in the **raw** reads
  (the strip rule) to take the board's own counter — no row yet refuses with a toast
  rather than writing attempt 1 over a real 3.
- Every card gets a quiet **Log attempt** button (`logAttemptHandler`, `dial: false`,
  no phone required — an attempt against a number-less patient is still an attempt).

Pinned by `components/careCoordinator/logAttemptWiring.test.ts`. The SOP's buttons
