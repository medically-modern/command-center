### 5.9 Chase Clinicals split — Fax vs "Email, Parachute & Dashboards"
The Confirm-Receipt→Chase step has **one Monday stage** ("Chase Clinicals" on the Masheke board)
but is sliced into **two app roles** by the **Clinicals Method** status column
**`color_mm1xw7y5`** (live labels: **`Fax` · `Parachute` · `Email` · `Dashboard`**, plus blank):
- **`chaseFax`** (`/chase-fax`) — method **`Fax` or blank** (a missing method counts as fax so
  nobody falls through the cracks).
- **`chaseParachute`** (`/chase-parachute`, labelled **"Chase Clinicals — Email, Parachute &
  Dashboards"**) — method **`Parachute`, `Email` or `Dashboard`**. **Email deliberately rides with
  Parachute** for queueing and cadence, but it is still **sent by email** (the optional fax/email
  re-send box keys off the panel's `roleMethod`, so Email patients in this role never see the fax
  re-send path).

⚠️⚠️ **THERE ARE TWO GROUPINGS AND THEY DIFFER BY `Email`. Do not merge them.** `Dashboard`
(Sept 2026, label id 3) is a referring practice reading its own patients in a partner dashboard —
District Endocrine today, the `Endochrine-dash` repo — and is **Parachute in every respect**
(Josh, 2026-09-18: *"however we treat a parachute method is how we are treating dashboard
methods"*). But "which queue works them" and "who carries the request" are different questions:

| | Fax | Email | Parachute | Dashboard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **QUEUE** — `chaseMethods.isParachuteRoleMethod` | fax | **parachute** | parachute | **parachute** |
| **DELIVERY** — `requestDelivery.isPortalMethod` | we send | **we send** | portal | **portal** |

- **`lib/masheke/chaseMethods.ts`** owns the queue: routing, cadence and counting. Its header says
  it decides the queue and **never the UI** — anything that looks different for a Dashboard patient
  keys on that patient's own `clinicalsMethod === "Dashboard"`, which is what keeps the Fax,
  Parachute and Email screens byte-identical.
- **`lib/masheke/requestDelivery.ts`** owns Send Request behaviour: a **portal method** gets no
  Generate Scripts step, a **collapsed** composer, and **skips Confirm Receipt** — it advances
  straight to Chase Clinicals at +3 business days on a **"Request Sent"** button the rep presses to
  confirm they sent it in the portal. There is deliberately **no auto-advance and no Monday column
  gating it**: the single button Parachute has always had.
- ⚠️ **`Email` is in the first and not the second.** Routing Send Request off the queue list would
  drag Email in and **silently stop every Email patient passing through Confirm Receipt** — no
  error, just a stage nobody enters. `sendRequestFlow.test.ts` fails the build if the panel ever
  imports `isParachuteRoleMethod`.

⚠️ **`lib/shared/partnerDashboard.ts` is the ROSTER URL, not a per-patient link** — the dashboard
keys patients on a hash of the Patient UID computed in another repo, so mirroring it here is the
§5.7/§5.17 hazard with a 404-on-a-live-call as its failure mode. Both the Send Request method badge
and the Chase panel's button link there; the rep signs in and finds the patient by name, which is
on screen beside the button. ⚠️ The href must stay a plain constant — a browser only allows a new
tab during user activation, and an `await` before it spends that activation.

This grouping is applied in **six** places that must stay in agreement — if you "fix" one,
fix all six (a future Claude keeps wanting to put Email back with Fax):
1. **Role page** — `src/pages/ChaseClinicalsPage.tsx` (the `useMemo` patient filter + header label).
2. **Role counts / bars** — `src/hooks/useRoleCounts.ts` ("Chase Clinicals" bucket → `isParachuteRoleMethod`).
3. **Oversight charts** — `src/lib/oversight/oversightApi.ts` `CHART_FILTERS`: **ten** chase entries,
   all reading the one `PARACHUTE_ROLE_METHODS` constant (`chase-fax` = method NOT in it,
   `chase-email-parachute` = method IN it; same split on the Escalations · Attempt 4+ row, ANDed
   with MN Attempts `color_mm1wz0vg` = `Escalate`, and on the 3rd-round, merged and proposed-stuck
   rows). ⚠️ One constant rather than ten literals, so a method added to the board cannot reach
   nine filters and miss the tenth.
4. **Doctor Appointments return** — `src/lib/masheke/apptOutreach.ts` `chaseRoleFor` (§5.12).
5. **Baseline (build time)** — `scripts/snapshot-baseline.mjs` `countMashekeStages` (was regressed
   to Email→chaseFax once; see §5.8 counting contract).
6. **Baseline (9 AM cron)** — `services/baseline-cron/index.mjs` `countMashekeStages`.

⚠️ Places 5 and 6 are plain Node and **cannot import the module**, so they carry the list as a
literal. **`chaseMethodMirror.test.ts` scans both and fails the build when either the list or the
`.includes(cm)` bucketing drifts** — verified to fail. Without it the drift is silent: the patient
is counted in the wrong bar all day and the burndown simply disagrees with the sidebar.

**Cadence:** on Complete the Next Action Date moves **+3 business days for every Clinicals Method**
(Fax/Email/Parachute/blank) — `ChaseClinicalsPanel.tsx` `nadBumpDays`. `config.ts`
`chaseFax`/`chaseParachute` are the role registry entries.

**Both chase roles carry the shared stamped NotesPanel** (2026-08-21). MN Workflow Notes were
render-only on this panel, so a rep who learned something on a chase call that wasn't an attempt
*outcome* had nowhere to put it — it went into the attempt note, where it reads as the outcome, or
nowhere. It is now `components/masheke/NotesPanel` (`variant="mm-inline"`) writing
`COL.mnEvalNotes` through `lib/shared/noteStamp`, identical to Evaluate / Send Request.
⚠️ `notePrefix` is **"Chase Clinicals" on BOTH roles**, not per-method: they are ONE Monday stage
sharing ONE notes column, the fax vs email/parachute split is already recorded by Clinicals Method,
and `NotesPanel`'s `ATTEMPT_LABEL_REGEX` bolds that exact string — `"Chase Clinicals — Fax:"`
matches neither. ⚠️ Adding a note writes **straight to Monday** and is deliberately **not** part of
the chase transaction: it does not gate "Chase Clinicals Completed", which still keys on the
attempt note in step 2. Those are different records — running case history vs. this attempt. The
panel passes **no `profileSendOffNotes`**, because `PriorStageNotes` directly above already renders
it (passing it would print the prior stage twice).
