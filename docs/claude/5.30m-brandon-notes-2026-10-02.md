**4. The ten-minute heads-up: a distinct two-ring chime and a top-right card.** Brandon asked for 2 minutes; **Josh chose 10** (`REMINDER_LEAD_MIN`, unchanged): *"a distinctly different ring that goes off 10 mins before call … just have it ring twice. and a pop up in the upper right like with calls saying who the call is with and when its scheduled for, only show this to people assigned care coordinator."*
- **Who sees it:** still gated in `ScheduledCallHost` to `access.type === "processor"` holding `scheduledCalls`. Managers don't get it, and neither does anyone in bootstrap mode (§5.15's caveat stands). A dual manager+processor resolves as a manager and doesn't get it.
- **The card:** `components/scheduledCalls/ReminderCards.tsx` shows the name, Intake/Welcome call, the ET time, "in N min" (live), the phone, Open and Dismiss. It is drawn **inside `IncomingCallHost`'s top-right stack**, under any ringing call, so the two kinds of card never overlap. IncomingCallHost sits **outside the router**, so Open navigates through `reminderStore.open`, using the `navigate` that `ScheduledCallHost` (inside the router) registers. A card drops 15 minutes after its start time, and at ET midnight. It replaced the old top-centre sonner toast.
- **The sound:** `lib/scheduledCalls/reminderChime.ts`. It is a falling E6 → C6 "ding-dong" on a triangle wave, played exactly twice. The incoming ring is a rising C5-E5-G5 sine chime that repeats while the call rings. Both are scheduled on the audio clock with no timer (`ringtone.ts`'s header explains why). The chime is primed on the first click or keypress. **Every open tab shows the card, but only one tab rings**: `claimChime` claims the call's key in localStorage, and storage that can't be used means that tab rings, because a doubled sound beats a missed one. The incoming-call mute (the speaker icon) doesn't silence it. Nobody asked for that.
- **Welcome calls are covered too.** Intake calls come from the monday mirror as before. Welcome calls exist only in Calendly, so the host also reads `/calendly/day?kinds=welcome` through the gateway every 2 minutes. The gateway caches a day for 60 seconds with an in-flight dedupe, so Calendly sees at most one read a minute however many tabs poll. Only `welcome` is asked for, because asking for intake too would announce every intake call twice. A failed read keeps the last good list. A welcome card's Open goes to `/care-coordinator`: the chart match happens there (§5.30l), not in the host.
- Tests: `components/scheduledCalls/callReminders.test.tsx` covers the card, the ten-minute window, the role gate, welcome calls, dismiss, the cross-tab claim, the two-ring/triangle/no-timer scan, and the card being inside the call stack.
