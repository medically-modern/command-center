### 5.13c Each person's own RingCentral line in Command Center (Sep 2026)
Josh, 2026-09-30: *"we want someone to call, everyone has their own sign in to void the 5 user
katie sign in cap and everyone gets rung in cc"*, then, the same afternoon: *"nothing in command
center should change accept the incoming call"*, *"assigned answers now shifts to whos logged in
on rc at that moment"* and *"its key that in the settings menu, anyone can sign into their rc
account"*. This is §5.13b's Route A, built without the call queue it planned.

**The rule, in one line: connecting your own RingCentral login in the settings menu IS being a call
answerer, and it changes ANSWERING only.**
- **Incoming:** rung in Command Center = connected (settings gear → Calls → *Connect my own
  RingCentral line…*). Your browser registers on YOUR extension, so none of Katie's five is spent.
- **The /access assignment is retired.** The "Answers calls" chip, its N-of-5 count and the header
  count are gone; `callAnswerers[]` is still in access.json and accessStore but nothing reads it for
  ringing. Somebody who has not connected is not rung, whoever they are. Samantha and Masheke have no
  RingCentral user, so they are not rung until they get one (Josh chose this, 2026-09-30).
- **Outgoing calls are untouched.** They go out on the shared line exactly as before: same caller ID,
  same call log, the one the archive and call history read. A connected person drops their own
  registration for the length of the call (`dialingShared` in `softphone.ts`) and gets it back when
  the call ends. Texting, faxing and everything else are untouched: they use the gateway's JWT.
- **Disconnected means told at once, as before.** Everything that watched an assigned answerer now
  watches a connected one: the red phone icon and the gear's dot, the bottom-left "Browser answering
  is off" notice, the `/access` board (the gateway adds everyone connected, `connectedEmails()`), and
  the ntfy escalation (which never needed the list: it watches every browser that reports).

**The problem.** Every browser provisioned its softphone with the gateway's one JWT, which belongs
to Katie's extension (ext 2), so the whole team shared her **five** SIP registrations (§5.13b). The
RingCentral desktop app signed in as Katie spent the same five, which is the throttling the team
kept hitting ("line full").

**What the RingCentral side is (set up 2026-09-30, in the admin portal, no code).**
- Staff users that already existed: Janelle (ext 7), Masani (ext 8), Victor (ext 13), each on
  RingEX with a Digital Line attached to the RingCentral App. No seats were bought.
- **Katie's line rings them directly**: Users → Katie Tyler → Phone → Incoming call rules →
  Incoming calls → Ring settings → *Add number or coworker* → each of them (ring all together).
  Katie's greeting, Classical connecting music and voicemail are unchanged, and unanswered calls
  still land in **Katie's** voicemail (verified with a test call).
- ⚠️ **The call-queue attempt failed and is switched off.** A custom rule on Katie ("Main line to Temp
  Ring All", *calls to (347) 503-7148 → queue ext 15*) sent a real call straight to voicemail: the
  queue treats every device signed in as Katie as ONE agent, so one rep on a call made "Katie" busy,
  and the other members were still signed in as Katie, so there was nobody available. Katie's line
  has call waiting on, and the queue took that away. The rule is **off** and the queue **Temp Ring
  All** (ext 15) is unused. The eight phone-tree queues (ext 4, 5, 6, 9–12, 14) were never touched.
- ⚠️ **Automatic recording is per user.** Only Corey, Katie and Stephen were being recorded; Janelle,
  Masani and Victor were added on 2026-09-30 (Phone System → Company → Sites → Main Site → Call
  recording → Automatic → Extensions to Record). A call Victor answered before that has no recording.

**What the code does.**
- **Gateway, `services/monday-gateway/rcUserAuth.mjs`** (pure half: `rcUserAuthRules.mjs`):
  `POST /rc/user/connect` (signed in) returns RingCentral's sign-in address, with a **signed, 15-minute
  `state`** that carries who clicked. `GET /rc/user/callback` exchanges the code, **refuses a login
  from any other RingCentral account** (compared with the JWT's account, failing closed), and stores
  the grant in `rc_user_links` on the **messaging** Postgres, keyed by the Google email.
  `GET /rc/user/status` and `POST /rc/user/disconnect` complete it.
- **`/messaging/sip-provision?line=own`** is the ONLY request that can get a person's own line
  (`provisionOwnLine(who)`, `mmLine: "own"`). Anything else, including `?line=shared` for outgoing
  calls and every browser on an app that predates this (no `line` at all, e.g. prod before its
  sync), gets the shared line exactly as before (`mmLine: "shared"`). The sip-provision floor still
  runs first (`sipProvisionFloor.test.mjs`). The browser refuses a shared answer to an own request
  rather than register on Katie's line, and re-reads the status.
- **SPA, `lib/softphone/rcLine.ts`**: the status, read once per page load and again after a
  connect, disconnect or sign-in, **never on a timer**. Other tabs of the browser hear about a change
  through the `mm-rc-line-changed` storage key. A FAILED read keeps the last status this browser was
  told for this person (`mm-rc-line-status`), never "not connected". `phoneLine(authOff, state)` is
  the one gate, used by both `IncomingCallHost` and `useCallStatus`: connected → rung on `own`;
  anyone else → not rung; a build without Google sign-in → everyone on `shared`, for local testing.
- **`softphone.ts`** takes `setLine` before `setEnabled`, remembers which line the live registration
  is on (`wpLine`), and re-registers on a change, but never under a live call (`endActive` reconciles
  afterwards; a change that lands mid-registration is reconciled once `registering` clears). The
  sipInfo cache (`registration.ts`) keeps the shared line under the key it always had and the own
  line beside it (`sipInfoKeyFor`), so neither overwrites the other.
- **Where people click:** settings gear → Calls → *Connect my own RingCentral line…*, offered to
  everyone signed in (the redesign shell has no home badge), or the home badge's *Connect RingCentral
  to take calls* on the old layout. *Disconnect my RingCentral line* is in the same menu and is disabled during
  a call. A successful return toasts "RingCentral connected".

**⚠️ Connected to the SHARED extension itself = the shared line (2026-09-30).** Josh, with no
RingCentral user of his own, connected with Katie's login, so his "own" line was ext 2, the very
extension the gateway's JWT provisions. His browser then held two different sets of SIP credentials
for one extension under one `instanceId` and swapped them for every outgoing call. It ended in a
refuse → re-provision loop (the log showed 200/304 provisions and the 8s floor's 429s), with a call
answered in silence both ways. Katie connecting her own login would do the same. Now
`isSharedExtension` (rcUserAuth.mjs) sends such a person down the shared path, `/rc/user/status`
reports `sharedLine: true`, and `phoneLine` keeps them on `shared` for everything: exactly the
browser phone as it worked before §5.13c, and no switching. The sip-provision floor is also keyed by
line, so an outgoing call's shared provision is never refused as a repeat of the own one. An auth
refusal now names RingCentral's SIP code, e.g. "(SIP 403)".

**⚠️ The shared incoming-call card is for the MAIN LINE only (2026-10-01).** The telephony webhook is
account-wide, so it carries every call on the account: a person's own direct number, a phone-tree
queue, a fax line. Before this, all of them popped a card on every screen. Once people answered on
their own lines, a card for a call that never reached Katie's extension (ext 2) showed *"This call is
ringing on another registered device"* with no Answer: it wasn't ringing that browser (Josh,
2026-09-30; the caller was in neither Katie's call log nor her fax inbox). `handleEvent` now drops a
new session unless one of its parties is on the shared extension (`callRules.ringsSharedExtension`,
the id from `rcUserAuth.sharedExtensionId()`), recording `kind: "other_line"` in `call_events`. A call
to someone's own number still rings them through their own registration. Blank means unknown: with
no extension id to compare, or no party naming one, the card shows. Sessions already on screen are
handled before the gate, so a later event that names only a coworker's leg can't strand a card. The
shared id is read once, one request at a time, and a failed read is retried at most every 5 minutes
(it is asked on every event), and awaited before `handleEvent` reads the calls map, so two events of
one session can't both look new and ring twice.
⚠️ **Not yet proven on live traffic (2026-10-01):** a card for a call that never reached Katie's
log ((412)…9071) still showed after this shipped. `GET /calls/health` → `lines` lists the last 40
decisions (verdict `shared` / `other` / `blank` / `no-id`, party statuses, how many parties name an
extension, no numbers) for new sessions and for later events on a shown card. Read it before
changing the rule: a `blank` first event that turns `other` later means the first notice doesn't
name the extension yet.

**Rules that are correctness, not style (pinned by `rcUserAuthRules.test.mjs` and `rcLine.test.ts`):**
- ⚠️ **A SECOND RingCentral app.** The developer console allows ONE auth flow per app, and the
  gateway's app is JWT. Texting, faxing, the call webhook and every archive sign in with it, so
  switching that app to the authorization-code flow would take all of them down. The per-person app is
  `RC_USER_CLIENT_ID` / `RC_USER_CLIENT_SECRET` (3-legged authorization code, refresh tokens on,
  scopes VoIP Calling + Read Accounts, redirect `https://monday-gateway-production.up.railway.app/rc/user/callback`).
  Until those two variables are set on `cmd ctr server`, status says `configured: false`, no Connect
  button shows anywhere, and nothing changes.
- ⚠️ **`/rc/user/*` is registered BEFORE `registerRingCentral`.** Its `app.all(/^\/rc\/.+/)` proxy would
  otherwise answer the callback 403, and the callback's address is registered with RingCentral, so the
  path cannot move.
- ⚠️ **Refresh tokens are sealed (AES-256-GCM) before they touch Postgres**, and access tokens stay in
  memory. The key is `RC_USER_TOKEN_KEY`, or HKDF of `PHONE_HMAC_PEPPER` under its own label (so it
  needs no new secret). **Rotating the pepper therefore disconnects everybody**; they connect again.
- ⚠️ **A dead grant is NOT put back on Katie's line.** Provisioning answers 409 "connect again" and the
  badge turns red with *Reconnect*. Falling back would quietly spend one of her five, which is the
  problem this exists to end.
- ⚠️ **RingCentral rotates the refresh token on every refresh**, so refreshes are one at a time per
  person. A keep-alive every 6 hours refreshes any grant with under 4 days left, because the browser
  caches its SIP credentials for 7 days and may not ask the gateway for anything all week.
- ⚠️ **Nothing registers until the status is in** (`phoneLine`).
- ⚠️ **Refresh writes are conditional on the grant they started from**, so a refresh that finishes
  after a reconnect or disconnect cannot overwrite the new grant, break it, or revive a deleted link.
- ⚠️ **A missing links table, or a failed lookup, never takes the shared line down**: the module is
  "not configured" until its table exists, and a lookup fault falls through to the shared path.
