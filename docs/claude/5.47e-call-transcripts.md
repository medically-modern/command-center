### 5.47e Call transcripts (Google Speech-to-Text, Oct 2026)
Josh, 2026-10-01: transcripts of calls, like the RingCentral app's AI Notes. RingCentral won't hand
those over (the AI Notes endpoint needs `ReadCopilotCallNotes`, an internal permission, §5.13c), so the
recordings the call archive already stores (§5.47) are transcribed by **Google Cloud Speech-to-Text**.
Google Cloud BAA accepted by Josh on 2026-10-01.

**Google side** (project `command-center-499519`):
- Bucket `mm-call-transcribe` (us-central1, public access prevented, soft delete off, lifecycle: delete
  objects older than 1 day). A drop-box only: each recording is copied in, transcribed, deleted.
- Service account `transcriber@…` with **Cloud Speech Client** on the project and **Storage Object
  Admin** on that bucket only.
- Railway variables on `cmd ctr server`: `GOOGLE_STT_CREDENTIALS` (the JSON key), `GOOGLE_STT_BUCKET`,
  `GOOGLE_STT_PROJECT`, and **`GOOGLE_STT_ENABLED=1`** — the switch. Nothing runs without it.

**Measured, not assumed (2026-10-01, with generated silence — no patient audio):**
- ⚠️ **Speaker separation works ONLY with BatchRecognize + model `chirp_3` + location `us`**
  (`us-speech.googleapis.com`). us-central1 and global refuse diarization; `long`, `telephony`,
  `chirp_2`, `short` refuse it everywhere; `chirp_3` doesn't exist in us-central1. A regional location
  also needs the regional host (`{location}-speech.googleapis.com`), or Google answers 400.
- A batch job took ~3.5 minutes end to end, mostly queueing. Transcripts appear minutes after a call.
- RingCentral recordings are **mono MP3, 32 kHz** — both voices on one channel — so "who said what"
  is Google's guess ("Speaker 1/2", numbered by who spoke first). It can't know which is the rep.

**What the code does** (`services/monday-gateway/callTranscribe.mjs`, pure half `callTranscribeRules.mjs`):
- Every 2 minutes: polls running jobs, then starts up to 4 new ones (max 12 running) for archived
  calls with `audio_state = 'stored'`, not faxes, **≥ 10 s**, started in the **last 3 days** (Josh,
  2026-10-01: "just do transcripts for the past 3 days … no need on calls less than 10 seconds";
  `GOOGLE_STT_LOOKBACK_HOURS` / `GOOGLE_STT_MIN_SEC` change it — a wider window is where cost is).
- Audio goes from OUR bucket to Google's directly; it never passes through anything else.
- Columns on `call_archive` (messaging DB): `transcript_state` (running/done/empty/failed),
  `transcript_json` ({speakers, turns:[{speaker,start,text}]}), `transcript_op`, attempts, error, times.
  3 failed starts → `failed`. A job unfinished after 3 h is abandoned and its copy deleted.
- `turnsFrom` handles both result shapes Google might send (labels per result, or the v1 style of one
  result holding every word) — the docs don't pin chirp_3's. **The first real transcripts are the
  check**: if `/calls/transcribe-health` shows `done` but the timeline shows one turn per call, the
  shape differs.
- Routes: `GET /calls/transcribe-health` (public: counts by state over 7 days, last error message,
  never text) · `GET /calls/transcript?callId=` (signed-in only; the turns).
- Timeline: `commsInbox` selects only `transcript_state`; the entry carries `hasTranscript`. The SPA's
  `CallTranscript` (under the recording in `ItemTimeline`) fetches the text when opened. Transcript
  text is never shipped with the timeline.

**PHI:** transcripts are patient speech. They live beside the recordings on the messaging DB, are read
only through the authenticated route, and nothing logs their text. The Google key was pasted into a
Claude session (Josh accepted that); rotating it later means a new key in Google Cloud + the Railway
variable.
