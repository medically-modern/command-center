### 5.53 The 2026-09-25 morning comms audit — four faults, one morning (Sep 2026)
Brandon's first end-to-end pass over comms, relayed by Josh: *"lets dive into comms and figure out
what was going wrong here, and any related issues with similar patterns"*. Four faults, each
MEASURED before it was fixed — the instruments are our own logs (Railway's HTTP log, `request_log`
§8, `call_events` §5.13), because RingCentral's records are what two of the faults are ABOUT.
**No board change; app + gateway.**

**1. "Setting up… → Hanging up…" for 20+ minutes — the SDK's call promises have no rejection
path.** Brandon dialed once, at 8:43:21 ET: the gateway saw his sip-provision land (200,
12:43:22Z) and RingCentral opened NO telephony session for the INVITE — zero webhooks
12:28→12:57Z on a stream that had carried Fidelis's calls minutes earlier. Read in
ringcentral-web-phone 2.5.1: `call()` awaits the INVITE's answer with **no timeout**,
`cancel()`/`hangup()` await a SIP reply the same way, and a SIP **403 makes `call()` RESOLVE**
with the session still in "init" and no `failed` event (outbound.ts returns early on the 403
status line) — §5.13b's REGISTER-hang class, extended to dial and teardown. So "Setting up…" had
nothing to end it, and Hang up traded it for "Hanging up…", equally for ever. Now in
`softphone.ts`:
- **`DIAL_PROGRESS_DEADLINE_MS` (15s)** — a watchdog on a dial still "connecting": fail with the
  reason, `dispose()` the session (**LOCAL teardown only** — a network goodbye to a dialog that
  never formed is another unanswerable request) and `recover()` (bounded, §5.13b), because an
  INVITE nothing answered indicts the SOCKET. Fire-and-check: the token + status re-checks make a
  stale timer a no-op, so nothing has to clear it, and a hang-up mid-dial spends the token.
- **The silent 403** is caught the moment `call()` resolves with the call still "connecting" —
  immediate, and NO recover: the socket demonstrably answered.
- **`HANGUP_DEADLINE_MS` (8s)** on the teardown await; a timeout disposes locally and recovers.
  ⚠️ An outbound call that never reached "answered" is torn down with **CANCEL, never BYE** —
  there is no established dialog to BYE, and on a session still in "init" `cancel()` throws
  synchronously (instant local cleanup), where `hangup()` would build a BYE from undefined
  headers and wait the whole deadline on it.
`callTeardown.test.ts` drives all five paths on the registrationDeadline fake-SDK harness; the
watchdog and the deadline were each verified to fail when removed.

**2. The rail said "Left voicemail"; the timeline said "Missed call" — and the timeline was
right.** Brandon called and hung up in the greeting on purpose. RingCentral's result `Voicemail`
means the voicemail SYSTEM answered, never that a message exists — the rail labelled off the
result (`callLabel`) while the timeline joins to the actual message store. `callLabel` now takes
**`hasMessage`**, fed by `PhonePanel`'s `vmMatched` memo built from **the same `voicemailForCall`
join the row's own click uses** (§5.28): true/false override the result, `null` (join not loaded)
falls back to it. ⚠️ Never a second rule — and the wiring scan in `callLabel.test.ts` pins the
RENDER site, because the default argument means every unit test passes with the join unwired.

**3. "We called · no answer" on a call the patient made — the §5.49 inversion, unanswered.**
The Fidelis timeline showed a phantom outbound at 8:26 AM between the 8:25 missed call and an
orphaned bare "Voicemail · 0:31" card. Proven, not guessed: exactly ONE `/comms/dialed` all
morning (Brandon's 8:43 dial), no second outbound telephony session in the webhook stream, and
the rail itself showing only three Fidelis rows — a genuine RC-app callback would have been a
fourth. RingCentral logged the unanswered, browser-rung, voicemail-taken inbound session as a
single **Outbound record toward the caller**, exactly as it does for a browser pickup (§5.49) —
and the deeper hole: `joinInbound` pairs voicemails with INBOUND calls only (hence the orphan),
and an inverted missed call that leaves NO voicemail **opens no inbox item at all** — a patient
who called and got nobody, absent from the queue built to catch them.
**`commsInboxRules.markInvertedInbound`** mirrors `markBrowserPickups`, evidence and all: our own
telephony webhook watched the real call ring and END unanswered (`call_events` kind=`end`,
state=`missed`, §5.13), so an outbound, UNCONNECTED, undialled record matching one of those ends
(session id, or hmac + `PICKUP_MATCH_MS`) is that call wearing the wrong direction →
`dir:"in", inverted:true` — after which the ordinary missed-call rules (the voicemail join,
`opensItem`, the chips) apply unchanged. Guards, each verified to fail when removed:
- `dialedBy` skips — a call somebody PRESSED CALL for is a genuine outbound;
- a missed end within the window of an INBOUND unconnected record is that record's own ending —
  never a warrant to invert a rep's genuine callback beside it;
- ⚠️⚠️ **`guardCalls`** — the LIST loaders SQL-pre-filter resolved (covered) inbound calls out of
  `events`, so the guard reads its own UN-covered query; without it a covered missed call's
  evidence would invert a rep's real callback and **REOPEN the item they resolved**;
- connected outbound never matches — the two rules partition on `callConnected`, and the end rows
  partition on state (missed → inversion, answered → pickups). A dropped webhook means no match
  and the old behaviour, never worse.
⚠️ **All THREE loaders, in lockstep** — `loadGroupAll` (the timeline), `loadInboundForList` (the
candidates ride the same cover clause + connected-labels pre-filter as the inbound arm; only a
candidate that really inverted joins the list) and `loadOpening` (what a resolve validates
against; its epoch-bound, un-covered inbound arm IS the guard there) — because an inversion the
list shows and the resolve's loader cannot see is a resolve validating against different events
than the rep was shown. `computeSnapshot` drops any `loadOutbound` row whose rc_call_id pass 1
already carries inverted, or one call renders twice. The evidence index
(`call_events_end_state_at_idx`, partial on kind=`end`) is created OUTSIDE SCHEMA behind
`to_regclass` — the table belongs to §5.13 and may not exist in a build without it.
⚠️ **Recorded, NOT fixed** (the same boundary §5.49 draws for pickups): surfaces reading
RingCentral directly in the browser cannot repair the inversion — the §5.28 contact marks,
`contact-totals` (counts the phantom as outbound), `CallHistoryList`'s outcome labels;
`end_unseen` rows carry no hmac, so only a session id can ever match them; left_vm's Listen has
minimal exposure (an unconnected call has no recording — it fails closed).

**3b. ROOT CAUSE, 2026-09-27 — RingCentral never inverted it; our archive froze a mid-call read.**
Josh, two days later: *"did we call them at 8:30 am friday"* — the Command Center still said so.
Read straight from RingCentral with the gateway's own JWT: the 8:26:44 call's record is **Inbound /
Voicemail** (`finished: true`, `lastModifiedTime` 8:28:20 ET, the voicemail message on its leg), and
**Friday's whole call log (80 voice records) has no Outbound record from an outside caller and none
that is Accepted or Voicemail** — the 18 inbound calls picked up on a SIP leg are all logged Inbound
too. The call log serves a call **while it is going** (`finished: false`, `result: "In Progress"`,
both in RingCentral's own CallLogRecord schema) and rewrites it when it ends; the 60-second tick
reads the last two hours, so it can catch one mid-call — and `call_archive`'s upsert **never updated
`direction`** after the first write (`toCallRow` also turns a missing direction into Outbound).
Fixed at the source: `callArchiveRules.isUnfinished` — an unfinished record is not archived at all
(the next tick reads the finished one) — and the upsert takes `direction = EXCLUDED.direction` from
every read, so the **deep pass (95 days, every ~20h) repairs every row already frozen wrong** with
no backfill script. NOT observed: the mid-call record itself (it is rewritten); what the §5.53
timeline showed — the phantom standing where the inbound 8:26 row belongs, nothing beside it — is
what says it kept the same id. ⚠️ **`contact-totals` reads `call_archive`, not RingCentral** (the
note above is wrong on that): the "We called N · They called M" counts and the Care Coordinator
cards counted the phantom from the same row, and are fixed by the same repair. `markInvertedInbound`
and `markBrowserPickups` stay as a net — they change nothing when the archive holds the true
direction — but a browser pickup "logged as Outbound" (§5.49) is probably this same freeze; not
re-measured. Tests: `callArchiveRules.test.mjs` (`isUnfinished`, and the upsert's `direction`),
each verified to fail with its half removed.

**4. A ~6s sip-provision metronome from one browser — INCIDENT_2026-08-20's shape, small.**
13:42:52→≥13:54Z one Edge/Windows browser re-fetched `/messaging/sip-provision` every ~6.0s: the
flat 5s auth retry PLUS the failure handler clearing the sipInfo cache, so every retry
re-provisioned — ~10 RingCentral calls a minute, each minting a device record (§5.13b), feeding
the real RC 429 on the call log at 9:48:49 AM ET. It stopped on its own; the shape is the
incident's. Two fixes, either alone insufficient:
- **The client ladder** — `retryDelayMs("auth", n)` is 10s · 20s · 40s · 60s cap, never a flat
  beat (`registration.ts`).
- **The gateway floor** — `SIP_PROVISION_FLOOR_MS` (8s) per caller+UA, 429 + `Retry-After`,
  checked BEFORE RingCentral is asked so no device record is minted; the map is bounded. The
  floor is what covers a stale tab running the old flat retry for days.
⚠️ **The floor must stay BELOW the ladder's first auth rung** (8s < 10s), or a genuine credential
recovery is refused by our own guard — `sipProvisionFloor.test.mjs` imports the real ladder and
pins the inequality; both files' comments name each other.

**Keep-in-agreement:** `retryDelayMs`'s auth ladder ⇄ `SIP_PROVISION_FLOOR_MS` (the inequality) ·
`callLabel`'s `hasMessage` ⇄ `voicemailForCall` (the row's click and its label are ONE join) ·
`markInvertedInbound` ⇄ `markBrowserPickups` (partitioned on `callConnected` + the end state) ⇄
