2

Your day, in order

  1. Check the day strip

    Those are appointments patients picked. They come first — a booked time is a promise.

  2. Work "Scheduled · Today", top to bottom

    The next call up is shaded darker. Be ready a few minutes before each block.

  3. Fill the gaps from "Unscheduled · Today"

    Longest-waiting first — the list is already in that order. These patients asked for a call but never picked a time.

  4. Open the patient to work them

    The blue Open button takes you to their profile. Calls, notes, logging the attempt, advancing — all of it happens there.

  5. Log every attempt

    No answer still counts — log it on the profile. The card's left edge turns green and the patient comes back on tomorrow's list automatically.

  • GREEN EDGE

    You've already tried this patient. Gray edge = not yet touched.

  • PILLS

    The facts at a glance: network verdict, form Partial/Completed, insurance, coverage paths. A missing pill means the answer is unknown — not "no".

  • CALL / COMMS

    Ring them, or open texts and call history in the side panel.

  • BOOKING LINK

    Send the calendar link so the patient picks a time — pick Intake call or Welcome call in the dropdown. The booking is what brings them back; never promise a callback without one.

  • OPEN

    The work button. It opens the patient's own page, where attempts get logged and stages advance. The dashboard itself never changes a patient.

4

How patients sort themselves

Each column is the same 2×2. Nothing to file — every patient lands in exactly one box on their own.

ScheduledToday Start here

A booked call on today's calendar. A booking always wins — it shows here no matter what else is going on.

ScheduledFuture

Booked for another day. Leave them be — they appear under Today on the right morning.

UnscheduledToday

No booking, and due: their follow-up date is today, past, or blank. Blank means call them — nothing else brings a dateless patient back.

UnscheduledFuture

You logged an attempt, so their follow-up date moved to the next day. They return to Today on their own.

Patients with a manager (escalated) leave the lists but stay in the column's count — managers work those from Oversight.

5

Five rules

Do

Work booked calls first, then longest-waiting.

Do

Log every attempt — answered or not.

The log is what schedules the next try.
Don't

Press Advance twice.

One press is enough. The patient disappears from your list the moment it goes through — if you ever see an "is already…" message, they had advanced; move on.
Don't

Treat a blank as a "no".

A missing pill or empty field means nobody has asked yet. Ask.
Do

Send the booking link instead of promising to call back.

A Calendly booking puts them on the day strip and holds the time. A verbal promise is invisible to every list.
