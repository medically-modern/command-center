3

What each button does

And what happens after you press it — nothing here is a mystery box.

On the card
  • 📞 Call→

    Rings the patient without leaving this page — the call goes out on the company number. The pop-up carries the attempt form, so you can fill in "What happened on the call?" while still talking. The call shows up in their history on its own.

  • ✍️ Log attempt→

    The same attempt form without placing a call — for right after you hang up. It's also inside the pop-up when you click a booking on the day strip.

  • 💬 Communications→

    A side panel opens on this card: the full text thread and call history in one place, and you can read and send texts from it.

  • 🔗 Booking link→

    Opens the calendar link to send the patient — the dropdown picks Intake call or Welcome call. When the patient books, the time appears on the day strip and their card moves under Scheduled on its own.

  • Open →→

    Opens the patient's own page — notes, logging an attempt, and Advance all live there. This screen is your view of the day; the patient's page is where the work happens.

Logging an attempt — wherever you do it
  • Log call attempt→

    Answered or not, three things are saved with the patient: your "What happened on the call?" note (it shows on the notes line at the bottom of their card), the "Follow up on" date, and one more on their attempt count. The card's left edge turns green and they slide to Unscheduled · Future until that date.

  • Advance→

    Everything you've entered is saved, and the patient's stage moves forward. Their card leaves your columns right away. One press does it — if you ever see an "…is already…" message, they had already moved on; nothing happens twice.

On its own
  • Nothing→

    The page keeps itself fresh on its own, about every minute — Refresh at the top does it right now. A patient who vanishes from a list has moved forward.

4

How patients sort themselves

Each column is the same 2×2. Nothing to file — every patient lands in exactly one box on their own.

ScheduledToday

A booked call on today's calendar. A booking always wins — it shows here no matter what else is going on. The next call up is shaded darker.

ScheduledFuture

Booked for another day. Leave them be — they appear under Today on the right morning.

UnscheduledToday

No booking, and due for a call: their follow-up date is today, already past, or blank. Blank counts as due — call them.

UnscheduledFuture

You logged an attempt, so their follow-up date moved to the next day. They return to Today on their own.

The lists sort themselves too: booked calls by their time, everyone else longest-waiting first.
