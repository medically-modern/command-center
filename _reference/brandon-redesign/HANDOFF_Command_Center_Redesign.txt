Command Center Redesign — Handoff for Josh
September 18, 2026 · Brandon Ellis · doc rev 34
What this is
This is a reorganization of the Command Center, not a rewrite. Everything the tool does today keeps working; what changes is how people get to it. Instead of separate Subscription, Orders, Update Clinicals and Communications pages that each start with their own patient lookup, there is one global search in the header and one patient screen that holds all of it: an Onboarding view (the four stages, an info strip, and a read-only snapshot of each sub-stage tool), a Subscription view (Profile | Orders) and a texts/calls column on the right. The 20 stage bars, the processor home and the stage tool pages are untouched. New per-user abilities (patient communication, adjust orders, edit profile, view others' views, reports, inventory) gate the actions that used to be their own pages.
Scope, in one line: the workflow UIs stay exactly as they are. This document is about navigation, the patient screen (profile and orders), the inbox, the Fax bar's right pane, Users/Settings and the custom home views. It is not a redesign of the stage tools (Unverified Referrals, Evaluate, Send Request, Confirm Receipt, Chase, Doctor Appointments, Benefits, Submit Auth, Auth Outstanding, DVS, Welcome Call, Final Confirm). Where the mockup shows one of those tools — behind a bar, or as a snapshot on the Onboarding view — it is a stand-in built from the deployed build's labels and cards, not a spec: keep the real components, and render them read-only where the snapshot calls for it.

What stays the same
Nothing in this list needs code changes; it is here so the scope is clear.
The 20 stage bars and the Stages (placeholder) view. Bars come from the access config exactly as today (processors[email].roles, roleOrder, roleFilters non-escalated / escalated / all), sized by queue count, click-through to the role's stage tool, 60-second refresh, the "Done!" state at zero. Managers still get the escalated-only banner. In the real-data build the counts are recomputed from the snapshot's board groups and stage-advancer values, so they are the counts as of Sept 18, not live.
The stage tool pages (/stage/:roleId): patient list on the left, the step-by-step form on the right, Save / Reset / Propose Stuck, See Referral Email. They are not part of this redesign and must not change. The mockup's versions of them are placeholders (step outlines from the deployed build, with a sample card or two) so navigation could be tested end to end — they are deliberately not accurate and nothing in them should be ported. The only new things are that the patient screen deep-links into them ("Open Evaluate", "Open Benefits" …) with ?patient=<id> preselected, and that the Onboarding view shows each tool read-only as a snapshot (see Onboarding view). One exception is described under What stays the same › Fax Inbox: the Fax bar page gains a right-hand pane.
Access model. Google sign-in restricted to the company domain, managers[], processors{}, callAnswerers[]. The redesign adds admins[], the custom-view field and the per-person abilities (see User management, settings & custom views).
Fax Inbox (the FAX bar) stays a bar for Masheke (fax role → /fax-inbox), and becomes two panes split half and half. Left: the faxes exactly as the inbox lists them today — a page-preview tile, office, number, city, pages, received time, Read / Unread, View and Download on the row, Load more; click a fax to select it. Right: the Update Clinicals flow, which is Josh's new Update Clinicals page moved into this screen — it replaces the Update Clinicals button, which is gone from the header and the dashboard. The pane starts with the selected fax's details, then "Find a patient" (search by name, 25 results with the board chip Subscription · <status> or Med Necessity · <sub-stage> and the DOB; plus "Likely matches" — patients whose doctor's fax number is the number the fax came from), then the patient header (name, board chip, DOB, and Medical Records + expiry for Subscription patients), a Clinicals on the Monday item card (the item's files with View, Attach this fax as clinicals, or drop another file), and then the work cards, exactly as Josh built them: for a Subscription patient, Update Visit Date (date → "New MN Expiry: <date + 6 months> · MR → MR Valid" → Save Visit Date) and the collapsible Office replied — no new records ("Use this when the fax came back without new clinicals": Same old records — the office sent what they already had, with an optional next doctor appointment, "we'll ask the office again the day after this unless the records have been updated by then", or "that date has already passed, so no follow-up will be scheduled from it"; New provider with optional details; Other with a required "what they said"; a "Goes in as: …" preview; Record reply writes the MR Request Log line and the Next Doc Appt Date); for a Medical Evaluation patient, the Submit — back to Evaluate card (Stage Advancer → Evaluate MN whatever the stage, escalation and stuck-proposal flags cleared, next action date today, so they reappear in the MN Evaluation queue). Send back to Evaluate therefore lives only here, inside that flow; it is not on the patient profile. Every patient screen still has the Faxes link in the texts column that opens this inbox filtered to that patient.
RingCentral — the call-answering chip moved into Settings, but the integration itself (inbound and outbound SMS threads, call log, recordings with download, click-to-call) is unchanged and is what the patient screen's Texts | Calls column and the hub read.
Monday as the system of record. Every field on the patient screen is a Monday column on one of the boards listed under Data & search index; the mockup does not introduce any new store. Writes that exist today (Save visit date, Upload clinicals, Send back to Evaluate, Replace infusion set, Send text) keep their existing gateway mutations; they are only relocated.
Auth Denied now opens the Auth Outstanding tool (/stage/authDenied renders the Auth Outstanding steps for the denied patients) — Brandon's call "for now"; the bar is clickable and the patient screen's Auth Denied sub-step links there.
New shell: the global header
The navy dashboard header becomes a persistent app header on every page, and it is the only place navigation lives — every button that used to sit around the screen (ad-hoc tiles, System Management, Manage Access, the RingCentral chip, the floating gear) folds into it. It has two kinds of things, and they look different on purpose. Sections are tabs: My Dashboard · Communications · Inventory · Reports & Metrics sit right after the brand as primary navigation — text tabs with an icon, the current one marked with a light-blue underline, a red unread count on Communications — because they are parts of the site you move between, not actions. Tabs a person can't use aren't drawn: Inventory needs the Inventory ability, Reports & Metrics the Reports ability, so a processor sees two tabs and Brandon, Josh and Katie see four. Then the global search, stretching to fill the middle. Utilities are buttons on the far right: Users (admins only, icon + label) and Settings ▾ (everyone; the gear carries a small dot with your call status). Under about 1250px the inactive tabs collapse to icons; on a phone the tabs drop to a second row under the search, full width, icon over label, like a bottom tab bar.

Onboarding view
A stepper of the four macro stages — 1 Intake, 2 Medical Evaluation, 3 Insurance, 4 Welcome Call — each card showing its state (done / in progress / not started / stuck), a progress bar (share of sub-steps done) and either the completion date or the current sub-step. Stages the patient hasn't reached are greyed out (dashed, muted, not clickable, tooltip "<stage> hasn't started — it opens once the patient gets there"); a step= deep link past the current stage falls back to the current one. Clicking a reachable card opens that stage below: one card per sub-step with a status pill (Done / In progress / Stuck / Not started), the fields that matter for that sub-step, Last touched, the escalation chip when the board's escalation column says so, and an Open <sub-step> link into the existing stage tool with this patient preselected. Under the cards: Notes from this stage — the board's notes column parsed into entries ([Mon D, YYYY, h:mm AM] text - INITIALS), newest first.
Which stage is current comes from the board the patient is active on (group not Completed / Archived), in order Send Off → Medical Evaluation → Insurance → Welcome Call; the sub-step comes from that board's Stage Advancer column. Rules the mockup applies: a stage the patient never had a board row for but is already past shows as done with the note "Completed before this patient was on the X board"; a later board row created early (e.g. a Subscription row while still stuck in Insurance) does not mark later stages done; a board group of Stuck marks the current sub-step stuck.
